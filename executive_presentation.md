- **Target Outcome:** Deploy a centralized, high-velocity CRM platform within 6 months.
- **Strategic Recommendation:** Procure and deploy **HubSpot Sales Hub Enterprise**.

## Slide 2: Client Problem & Operational Metrics

### The High Cost of Spreadsheet Fragmentation
- **150 Disconnected Reps:** No unified source of truth for deal tracking or account status.
- **4.2 Hours/Week Lost per Rep:** Manual data entry and spreadsheet reconciliation drains selling time.
- **18% Pipeline Leakage:** Unassigned leads drop through the cracks due to missing automation.

## Slide 3: Vendor Candidates & Market Positioning

### Evaluating the Three Market Contenders
- **Salesforce Enterprise:**
  - *Positioning:* The traditional heavyweight enterprise platform.
  - *Pros:* Infinite customization, massive developer ecosystem, complex CPQ rules.
  - *Cons:* High licensing cost ($150/user/mo), steep learning curve, requires dedicated full-time admins.
- **HubSpot Sales Hub Enterprise:**
  - *Positioning:* Modern, intuitive, high-adoption revenue engine.
  - *Pros:* Best-in-class UX, turnkey email/calendar sync, fast 6-month deployment.
  - *Cons:* Less specialized custom object depth for massive conglomerate structures.
- **Zoho CRM Enterprise:**
  - *Positioning:* Budget-friendly modular suite.
  - *Pros:* Lowest direct cost ($40/user/mo).
  - *Cons:* Clunky UI, fragmented module connections, high rep adoption resistance.
